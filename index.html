About Niki McElroy

Niki McElroy is a multifaceted creative professional whose work spans writing, producing, and acting. As the founder of A Flawless Film, LLC, she brings stories to life with a unique vision and unwavering commitment to quality.

With a proven track record of successful film productions, Niki leads an in-house team capable of handling every aspect of the filmmaking process — from initial concept and screenplay development through production, post-production, and final distribution.

Niki's credits include the powerful BET+ Original drama Soul of a Sister (premiering March 2025), the award-winning occult thriller Shady Grove (Best Feature winner at Days of the Dead Film Festival), and the horror-comedy Haunted House of Pancakes, all currently streaming on major platforms.
